CVFD app privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

The CVFD app is made by the Community Volunteer Fire Department Training Division for its members. It gives members SOGs, size-up references, training content and a personal training record.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us).
  • Training record you enter: rank, station, employee ID, certifications and expiration dates, training hours and phase book progress.
  • Cadre content entered by Training Division staff, such as weekly check-ins and testing schedules.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or analytics, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see it

Your training record is visible to you and to Training Division staff, who review it for certification and ISO tracking. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in the United States. A copy is kept on your device so the app works without signal.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Account and tap Delete my account. This permanently deletes your sign-in and your training record. You can also ask the Training Division to delete it.

Contact

Questions: contact the CVFD Training Division.
